# Threads of Greatness — KDP Book Description

**Title:** Threads of Greatness
**Subtitle:** Educating Children, Developing Talent, and Forming Lifelong Learners
**Authors:** Will & Donna Meier

---

## Plain-text version (paste into KDP if you don't want to use HTML)

What if the goal of education was not information — but formation?

We have been measuring the wrong things for too long.

A child who can recite facts but cannot recognize their own gifting. A student who scores well but doesn't know how to recover from failure. A teenager who is academically capable but emotionally untethered. A young adult who finished school but cannot navigate life.

The system is not broken because children are failing. It is broken because we have asked it to do something it was never designed to do: form whole people.

Threads of Greatness offers a different model — one rooted in equipping rather than mere instruction. Drawn from four decades of leadership formation, parenting, and developing leaders in more than 130 nations, Will and Donna Meier present nine practical enhancements any parent, teacher, coach, or leader can begin using this week.

Inside this book you will learn how to:

- Identify a child's natural gifting and passion before the world labels them
- Leverage multiple intelligences so no child is measured by the wrong instrument
- Walk a learner through adversity in a way that builds identity, not shame
- Shepherd the heart — the soil from which behavior, motivation, and character grow
- Understand different learning pathways without trapping a child inside one
- Activate creativity as an essential capacity, not a reward for finishing the "real" work
- Use humor and joy to build the psychological safety that deep learning requires
- Build together, forming collaborators and leaders instead of only solo test-takers
- Train critical thinking that produces wisdom, not cynicism

Then it shows you how to weave all nine into a simple weekly rhythm that forms the whole person, not just the report card. These principles reach far beyond the classroom. They transfer into the workplace, into ministry, into leadership teams, and into the way we keep forming ourselves.

Each chapter closes with six reflection and coaching questions — 72 in all — designed for parents, teachers, coaches, leaders, and your own ongoing formation.

This is education that forms more than grades. It forms the next generation.
